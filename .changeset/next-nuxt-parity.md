Bring the package on par with `@b10cks/nuxt`. `@b10cks/next/middleware` answers b10cks redirects in `proxy.ts` from a list cached per instance. `getRouteContent` on the server and `useContent` with `forRoute: true` on the client redirect or render `not-found`. `@b10cks/next/image` adds `B10cksImg`, and `@b10cks/next/client` adds `useB10cksVersion` and `usePageTranslations`.
