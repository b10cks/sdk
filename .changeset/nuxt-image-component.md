Add `B10cksImg` in `@b10cks/nuxt/image`. It renders a b10cks asset with `NuxtImg`, and `loading="eager"` adds high fetch priority and a preload link that matches the image's `srcset` and `sizes`. `@nuxt/image` 2.2.1 or newer is an optional peer dependency, needed only for this entry.
